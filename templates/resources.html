Renewable energy resources for the MESCOM area

Electricity in this area comes from MESCOM (Mangalore Electricity Supply Company). These options reduce what you draw from the grid.

☀ Rooftop solar

Best fit

About 4–5 peak sun hours a day on the coast; output dips in the June–August monsoon. Grid-connected systems with net metering export surplus to MESCOM. Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy: ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW (cap ₹78,000).

🚿 Solar water heater

Replaces a 2 kW geyser. Hot water from the sun cuts roughly 60 units a month in a family home.

💨 Small wind

Limited to windy coastal and ridge sites. Usually hybrid with solar; needs a site wind survey first.

🌿 Biogas

Kitchen and farm waste in Udupi and Malnad villages gives cooking gas and slurry manure, reducing LPG and power use.

💧 Micro-hydro

Streams in the Western Ghats foothills of Shivamogga and Chikkamagaluru suit small community schemes.

🏘 Community solar

Apartments and RWAs can share a rooftop or ground-mount plant through virtual net metering where permitted.

Gruha Jyothi & your bill

Under Gruha Jyothi, eligible households get units free up to their entitlement (average usage + 10%, max 200 units). Solar still helps: it lowers units above the free limit and earns export credit.
